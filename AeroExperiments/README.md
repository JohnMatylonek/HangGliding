# AeroExperiments Archive

This directory preserves technical writings by Steve Seibel originally associated with **aeroexperiments.org**.

The collection concerns hang-glider and aircraft flight mechanics, including:

- yaw–roll coupling;
- adverse yaw;
- dihedral and anhedral;
- sideslip and skid;
- washout and billow;
- flexible-wing geometry;
- turning-flight theory and observational experiments.

## Introductory Overview

For a clear introduction to the collection, read **[Steve Seibel’s AeroExperiments: Investigating the Hidden Relationship Between Yaw, Sideslip, and Roll](../SteveSeibel/Steve-Seibels-AeroExperiments.md)**.

## Archival Notes

The source files are preserved as plain text. Their substantive wording has not been modernized or silently corrected. Several describe themselves as early editions or works awaiting further revision and photographs.

The source files `Billow.txt` and `Experiments.txt` were compared during organization. They were identical, so one canonical copy is preserved here rather than publishing a duplicate.

These writings represent their dated editions and should be read in that historical context.
