# Hang Gliding

A collaborative repository for hang-gliding knowledge, instruction, history, and community scholarship.

This project preserves and develops:

- Articles by Steve Seibel and other contributors
- Instructional materials for pilots and instructors
- Technical discussions of hang-glider design, control, and flight
- Historical documents, photographs, and personal recollections
- Educational images, diagrams, and eventually videos
- Contributions from the wider hang-gliding community

## Purpose

Hang gliding has accumulated an extraordinary body of practical knowledge. Much of it is scattered across personal archives, aging websites, newsletters, informal discussions, and the memories of experienced pilots.

This repository provides a durable, organized place where that knowledge can be preserved, examined, improved, and shared. Contributions, corrections, competing interpretations, historical evidence, and carefully documented observations are welcome.

## Planned Sections

- **Articles** — essays, technical articles, and commentary
- **Instruction** — training and pilot-education materials
- **History** — historical records, photographs, and archival resources
- **Research** — experiments, observations, and technical analysis
- **Media** — images, diagrams, audio, and video
- **Contributors** — author biographies and attribution information

## Contributing

Contributors may propose additions, corrections, and revisions through GitHub issues and pull requests.

Authors retain attribution for their work. Inclusion in this repository does not imply that every contributor agrees with every technical interpretation or opinion presented here.

## Safety Notice

Hang gliding is an aviation activity involving substantial risk. Materials in this repository are educational and historical. They are not substitutes for qualified in-person instruction, appropriate certification, sound pilot judgment, current manufacturer guidance, or compliance with applicable laws and operating rules.
