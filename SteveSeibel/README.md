# Steve Seibel

This directory is the author and collection guide for materials by **Steve Seibel** in the Hang Gliding repository.

Steve's archived technical work explores the often-overlooked transitional relationships among yaw, sideslip, roll, and turn entry. His writings connect pilot observation and physical experimentation with questions involving adverse yaw, dihedral and anhedral, billow, washout, flexible-wing geometry, and yaw–roll coupling.

## Introductory Overview

Start with **[Steve Seibel’s AeroExperiments: Investigating the Hidden Relationship Between Yaw, Sideslip, and Roll](Steve-Seibels-AeroExperiments.md)** for an accessible account of the project’s questions, methods, developing conclusions, and significance.

## Archived Technical Writings

Steve's original AeroExperiments materials are preserved in the [AeroExperiments archive](../AeroExperiments/README.md).

The archive currently contains dated writings from 2005 and 2007, together with a longer theory compilation.

## Editorial and Attribution Policy

- Original writings are identified as Steve Seibel's work and retain their original dates.
- Archival texts are not silently rewritten to reflect later interpretations.
- Editorial introductions, commentary, or disagreements should be clearly distinguished from Steve's own words.
- Inclusion of later commentary in this repository must not imply Steve's endorsement.
- Additional biographical information, photographs, correspondence, or unpublished work should be added only when provenance and permission are reasonably clear.

This directory can later expand to include a biography, bibliography, photographs, videos, and other properly attributed materials.
